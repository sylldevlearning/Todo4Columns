# DoFirst Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 25, 2026

DoFirst is an Android task manager based on the Eisenhower matrix. This policy explains what the app does with your data. In short: **DoFirst collects no data and sends nothing to anyone.**

## No data collected or sent

- DoFirst does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal data.
- The app contains no analytics, usage statistics, advertising tracking or crash reporting tools.
- No DoFirst server receives your tasks or settings: there isn't one.

## No account

DoFirst works without an account, sign-up, email address or phone number.

## No ads

DoFirst shows no ads and does not use the device's advertising ID.

## Data stored on your phone

Your tasks, settings, the log of completed tasks (date and quadrant, without the title) and your weekly review history are stored only in the app's private storage, on your phone. No other app can access them.

You can delete everything at any time by clearing DoFirst's data in Android settings, or by uninstalling the app.

## Google's Android backup

If you have turned on your phone's backup (Android settings, Google account), Android may include DoFirst's data in that backup, as it does for other apps. This backup is managed by Google according to your settings and Google's own privacy policy; DoFirst has no access to it. You can turn it off in Android settings.

## Export files, only when you ask

When you tap "Export my data", DoFirst creates a backup file and opens the Android share sheet: you choose where to send it (Drive, email…). The temporary file is deleted the next time the app starts. Nothing is exported without your action.

When you import a backup, you pick the file yourself in the Android file picker; DoFirst reads only that file.

## No storage permission

DoFirst does not ask for access to your photos, files or storage. Storage permissions are removed from the app.

## Local notifications only

Reminders are local notifications: they are scheduled and shown by your phone itself, without going through the internet or a server. You can refuse or turn them off at any time in Android settings; your tasks stay saved.

## Sharing and messages

When you use "Send", "Follow up", "Share DoFirst" or "Suggest an idea / report a problem", DoFirst opens the app of your choice (messaging, email…) with ready-made text. Nothing is sent until you send it yourself from that app. DoFirst has no access to your contacts.

The suggestion email includes the DoFirst and Android versions, to help understand a problem; you can remove them before sending.

## Play Store reviews

From time to time, DoFirst may show Google Play's official window inviting you to rate the app. This window is provided by Google Play; any rating or review is handled by Google under its own privacy policy. DoFirst does not receive your rating.

## Children

DoFirst collects no data, including from children.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at the same address with its update date.
