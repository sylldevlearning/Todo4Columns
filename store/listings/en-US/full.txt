DoFirst is a simple, smart to-do list that helps you organize your tasks and manage your priorities with the Eisenhower Matrix. Task manager, reminders, calendar and time management in one app.

TWO QUESTIONS, THAT’S ALL
For each task, answer “Is it urgent?” and “Is it important?”. DoFirst puts it in the right box:
• Do: urgent and important, handle it now
• Schedule: important but not urgent, plan it
• Delegate: urgent but not for you, hand it off
• Someday: neither urgent nor important, no pressure

DICTATE YOUR TASKS
• Tap the mic and speak: your to-do list fills up without typing
• “Tomorrow at 3 pm” becomes a due date in one tap
• The words “urgent” and “important” suggest the right box

YOUR DAY AT A GLANCE
• Monthly calendar with dots in each box’s color
• A timeline for every day: due dates, steps and recurring tasks

MAKE PROGRESS STEP BY STEP
• Break a task into steps to check off, with a progress %
• No steps? Set the progress yourself from 0 to 100%
• Spread a project over sessions on the days you choose: consecutive days, Monday-Wednesday-Friday or specific dates
• Press and hold a task to check its steps in one gesture

REMINDERS THAT FOLLOW UP
• Notifications and follow-ups until an urgent task is done
• A reminder the day before or one hour before a deadline
• A scheduled task warns you when it becomes urgent, then moves to “Do” once it’s overdue
• Tap “Done” or “Snooze 1 h” right in the notification

PLAN YOUR WEEK
• “Your week” and the summary: see how much of your work you planned ahead
• Weekly review: 5 minutes to sort things out and pick 3 priorities
• Recurring tasks: daily, weekly, monthly…

DELEGATE WITHOUT FORGETTING
Send the task by SMS or WhatsApp in one tap, and follow up at the right time.

WHEN GETTING STARTED IS HARD
If you find it hard to get organized or to get going, including with ADHD, DoFirst keeps things simple: one question at a time, small steps, reminders and a to-do list that shows what matters first.

SIMPLE AND MOTIVATING
• Swipe right to complete, left to delete
• A small celebration for every task you finish
• Built-in help: an “i” explains every option
• Available in 13 languages

ON ALL YOUR PHONES
• Sync your tasks between several phones with Google Drive (optional)
• Home screen widgets: “Do”, “Today” and “Matrix”; check off a task without opening the app
• Light or dark theme, or automatic like your phone

YOUR DATA IS YOURS
No account, no ads. Your tasks stay on your phone, and you can export them to a backup file at any time. If you like, back them up to your Google Drive too, in a private folder only DoFirst can use, and get them back automatically on a new phone.

For work, school or home, DoFirst replaces your to-do list app, notes and daily checklist. Boost your productivity and stay focused: do what matters first.
